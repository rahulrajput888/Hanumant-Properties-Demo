01 Our story

Built on repeat clients, not advertising

The business started the way most neighbourhood businesses in Delhi do — one satisfied family telling another. No hoardings, no portal subscriptions, no call-centre follow-ups. Two decades later, a large share of our work still comes from people whose parents, brothers or colleagues we dealt with years ago.

That model only survives if the advice is honest. So we say no to properties we would not buy ourselves, we walk away from deals where the paperwork smells wrong, and we tell owners when their asking price is unrealistic — even when the easier thing would be to stay quiet and collect brokerage.

Sector-24 in Rohini is home base. We also work actively in Sector-7, 9, 14, 21 and 22, plus Prashant Vihar and Pitampura — enough coverage to find you options, small enough that we still know each street.

Our working promise

Brokerage is stated up front. Documents are checked before token money. If a deal has a catch, you hear it from us before you hear it from a bank, a lawyer or a neighbour.

03 What we stand on

Four rules we do not bend

Rule 01

Honest pricing

We quote the realistic market number, not the number that makes the deal sound exciting.

Rule 02

Papers first

Ownership, dues and approvals are checked before any token money changes hands.

Rule 03

One contact

The person who shows you the property closes it with you. No handoffs, no confusion.

Rule 04

No pressure

If it is not right, we say so — and we keep looking. A rushed client is never a repeat client.
