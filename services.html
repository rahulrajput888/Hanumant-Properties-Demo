02 The long version

How a deal actually moves with us

Six steps, in the order they happen. Most deals sit between step two and step five for two to four weeks.

01

Requirement call

Budget, purpose, locality, timeline and — importantly — what you do not want. Ten minutes on the phone saves ten wasted visits.

02

Curated shortlist

Three to five options, each with an honest note on what is good and what is a compromise. No filler listings to pad the list.

03

Site visits

Visits arranged around your schedule, including evenings and weekends. We walk the street with you, not just the flat.

04

Checks & negotiation

Documents, dues and measurements verified. Then the price conversation — handled by us, so you stay in a comfortable position.

05

Token & agreement

Token amount, agreement drafting and registration timelines coordinated with both sides and the draftsman.

06

Handover & after

Meter transfers, society formalities and possession. And yes — we still pick up the phone months later.
