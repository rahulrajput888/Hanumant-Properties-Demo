01 Read the numbers

Three things people miss in an EMI

The monthly figure is the easy part. These are the ones that change decisions.

01 · Tenure

A longer tenure lowers the EMI, not the cost

Stretching from 15 to 25 years can cut the monthly outgo noticeably, but the total interest usually grows by lakhs. Comfortable EMI and cheapest total rarely live together.

02 · Rate

Half a percent is not a rounding error

On a ₹50 lakh loan over 20 years, a 0.5% rate difference changes the total payable by several lakh rupees. Always compare the effective rate, not just the advertised one.

03 · Hidden costs

Processing, legal and insurance charges

Processing fees, legal and technical charges, and property insurance sit outside the EMI. Ask for the full sanction letter breakdown before you sign.

03 Loan journey

From eligibility check to disbursal

What we help with at each stage — and what only the bank can decide.

01

Eligibility & budget fit

Income, existing EMIs and tenure decide your comfortable borrowing range. We work out how much house that actually buys in your preferred sector.

02

Document preparation

Salary slips or ITR, bank statements, KYC, property papers — collected in the exact format the lender asks for, so the file does not bounce.

03

Application & sanction

Application filed through the bank channel. Sanction terms — rate, tenure, fees — are issued by the lender and reviewed with you before you accept.

04

Legal & technical

The lender's own legal and technical teams verify the property. We coordinate the paperwork so this stage does not stall the deal.

05

Disbursal & registry

Disbursal cheque or transfer, registry appointment, and the final paperwork — sequenced so both sides know exactly what happens on which day.
